# NeuroSkin Questions

This file lists every question used in the questionnaire module.
These questions are for screening only. They are NOT a medical diagnosis.

## Part 1: PHQ-9 (depression symptoms)

Source: Kroenke, Spitzer and Williams (2001). Official wording from
https://www.phqscreeners.com 
Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by the following problems?

Answers: 0 = Not at all, 1 = Several days, 2 = More than half the days, 3 = Nearly every day

1. Little interest or pleasure in doing things
2. Feeling down, depressed, or hopeless
3. Trouble falling or staying asleep, or sleeping too much
4. Feeling tired or having little energy
5. Poor appetite or overeating
6. Feeling bad about yourself, or that you are a failure or have let yourself or your family down
7. Trouble concentrating on things, such as reading or watching television
8. Moving or speaking so slowly that other people could have noticed, or the opposite: being so fidgety or restless that you have been moving around a lot more than usual
9. Thoughts that you would be better off dead, or of hurting yourself in some way

Scoring: total 0 to 27.
0-4 minimal, 5-9 mild, 10-14 moderate, 15-19 moderately severe, 20-27 severe.
Item 9 above 0 sets the safety flag (rule R6).

## Part 2: GAD-7 (anxiety symptoms)

Source: Spitzer, Kroenke, Williams and Lowe (2006). Same answers as PHQ-9.

Over the last 2 weeks, how often have you been bothered by the following problems?

1. Feeling nervous, anxious, or on edge
2. Not being able to stop or control worrying
3. Worrying too much about different things
4. Trouble relaxing
5. Being so restless that it is hard to sit still
6. Becoming easily annoyed or irritable
7. Feeling afraid, as if something awful might happen

Scoring: total 0 to 21.
0-4 minimal, 5-9 mild, 10-14 moderate, 15-21 severe.

## Part 3: Stress (written by the author, NOT validated)

In the last 2 weeks, how often did you feel this way?

Answers: 0 = Never, 1 = Rarely, 2 = Sometimes, 3 = Often, 4 = Very often

1. I felt that I could not control the important things in my life.
2. I felt under too much pressure.
3. I felt that problems were piling up and I could not deal with them.
4. I felt tense or on edge because of daily demands.

Scoring: total 0 to 16, divided by 16 to get a value between 0 and 1.
LOW below 0.34, MODERATE below 0.67, HIGH from 0.67.

## Part 4: Skin impact (written by the author, NOT validated)

In the last 2 weeks, how much did your skin affect you?

Answers: 0 = Not at all, 1 = A little, 2 = A lot, 3 = Very much

1. My skin made me feel embarrassed or self-conscious.
2. My skin made daily activities (work, study, housework) harder.
3. My skin affected my choice of clothes or going out and meeting people.
4. My skin itched, hurt or felt uncomfortable.

Scoring: total 0 to 12, divided by 12 to get a value between 0 and 1.
Same LOW / MODERATE / HIGH thresholds as stress.

## Notes for the report

- PHQ-9 and GAD-7 are validated instruments. The wording above follows the official version.
- The stress and skin impact questions were written by the author. They are not validated and the thresholds are simple thirds, not clinically tested.
- Results are screening-oriented only and do not diagnose any condition.
